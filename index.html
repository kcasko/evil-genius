YOUR FIRST ACT OF VILLAINY

Steal the Moon.
Keep the staff alive.

A turn-based game of questionable management. Build a secret lair, assemble a dysfunctional crew, and pull off a lunar heist.

Your goal: complete the Moon mission with at least 25 Infamy. Keep Heat below 100% or the authorities shut you down.

First move: build a Server Room ↓
How to play YOUR FOUR-STEP EVIL PLAN
  1. 01

    Build your lair

    Start with a Server Room ($250, 1 Command Point). It takes one day to build, then earns research and covers CHAOS's daily upkeep.

  2. 02

    Earn your Moon shot

    Start with Raid Rival Payroll, then unlock the satellite and shrink-ray jobs. Build the Server Room and Laboratory and research gear before the finale. One operation can launch each day.

  3. 03

    Run the heist

    Scout → Approach → Breach → Escape. Pick exactly two crew for Approach. The AI advises on Breach; the game roll decides success. Offline play uses a local fallback.

  4. 04

    Handle the fallout

    Resolve crises and rest stressed crew. You get 2 Command Points each day. Unspent points disappear when you end the day. Dawn brings income, upkeep, and possible trouble.

No reflexes required. Read the risk preview before committing. Even a STRONG chance can fail its roll. Progress saves in this browser; Export keeps a backup you can Import later. The first live AI decision can take up to 90 seconds.
